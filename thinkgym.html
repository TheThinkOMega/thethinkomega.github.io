About ThinkGym

One Platform for Your Entire Gym

ThinkGym is a comprehensive gym management application designed to streamline every aspect of your fitness facility operations. With three distinct profiles — Admin, Staff, and User — the app provides a seamless experience for managing your entire gym ecosystem.

Track staff members, monitor equipment, manage payments, and oversee all operations in real-time. ThinkGym offers advanced features including biometric staff attendance integration, automated equipment maintenance reminders, WhatsApp integration for instant communication, and comprehensive user tracking capabilities.

Admin Profile

Full control over gym operations, staff, and finances.

Staff Profile

Manage daily tasks, attendance, and member interactions.

User Profile

Track workouts, view schedules, and manage memberships.

Features

Everything you need to run a gym

Members, payments, attendance and more — handled in one app, so you can focus on your members.

Member Management

Add members, track memberships, and see who's active, expiring, or overdue — at a glance.

Payments & Billing

Record payments, track outstanding balances, and keep a clean history for every member.

Attendance Tracking

Automatic check-in logs give members and staff a complete, real-time attendance record.

Biometric Check-in

Integrated eSSL fingerprint readers let members check in without codes or cards.

Admin, Staff & Member

Three role-based profiles, each with the right tools and permissions for the job.

WhatsApp Reminders

Send renewal reminders and updates straight to members' WhatsApp in one tap.

Equipment Maintenance

Log equipment and get maintenance reminders so nothing goes down unexpectedly.

Real-Time Sync
